## Headline: the scale claims contradict each other across properties

The same company states materially different numbers depending on which audience is reading. Collected from this scan:

| Claim | Figure | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate clients | **"20K+ corporate clients"** | Provider portal → Mantra Premium |
| Organizations | **"Access 2000+ Organizations"** | Provider portal → Mantra Premium (same page) |
| Companies | **"50+ companies"** | mantracare.org/employers |
| Countries | **30+** | mantracare.com corporate site |
| Countries | **40+** | web.mantracare.com client login screen |
| Countries | **130+** | mantracare.org/employers |
| Individuals | 1M+ | Provider portal → Mantra Premium |
| Therapy providers | 15,000+ | web.mantracare.com/plans/therapy |
| Psychiatry providers | 5,000+ | web.mantracare.com/plans/psychiatrist |

**20,000+ corporate clients, 2,000+ organizations and 50+ companies cannot all be true** — and the first two appear *on the same page*. Country counts span 30 / 40 / 130.

This matters for two reasons. First, for your competitive benchmarking: **treat every MantraCare scale figure as marketing, not data.** Second, it is the same pattern the clinical layer shows — the shape of rigour without the substance. The number is chosen to suit the pitch in front of it.

## The offering

**`mantra.care`** is the corporate brand — "Global leader in Corporate wellness, EAP & Telehealth" — with **"Request Demo"** as the primary nav CTA. `mantracare.org/employers/` is a parallel employer landing page. Neither cross-references the other.

**Three packaged solution areas** (mantracare.org/employers):
1. **Mantra EAP** — mental health counselling, mindfulness/yoga, **financial wellbeing**, burnout assessment, life coaching
2. **Mantra Wellness** — virtual medical consultations, health screenings, nutrition, fitness/weight management, workplace challenges
3. **Mantra Specialized Care** — diabetes management, women's health, physiotherapy, substance abuse support, maternity/paternity

The fuller service list adds PCOS management, smoking cessation, corporate health checks, leadership coaching and stress management, delivered via **live video, chat, audio and in-person**, plus **"2,000+ articles, videos, self-assessments"** in a mobile app.

This confirms the Part 3 reading: the 58-type provider taxonomy (including Legal Counsellor and Financial Wellbeing Advisor) exists to service **EAP breadth**, where legal and financial support are standard components.

### Pricing
**No EAP price is published.** The `mantra.care/eap/eap-cost/` page is generic market-education content stating that "EAPs can cost anywhere from **$1 to $8 per employee per month**" (~$12–96 PEPY) and advising buyers to compare vendors — it does not state MantraCare's own rate. The motion is demo-and-quote.

This is the third distinct pricing model in the business:
1. **Provider SaaS** — USD $0/$49/$99/$149 per month, credit-metered
2. **Consumer** — geo-priced per session/subscription (₹260–₹6,280 / $35–$199)
3. **Corporate EAP** — unpublished, quote-based, likely PEPM

### Lead capture
Both properties run a lead form rather than self-serve: **"Book Now" / "Book a Free Consult"** collecting **name, company, email, phone, employee count, preferred programs**, and comments. Employee count as a qualifying field confirms PEPM-style pricing behind the quote.

This closes the loop with the provider-side **"Refer a Corporate"** programme — clinicians are offered **15–20% of total contract value** for introducing exactly these leads, with MantraCare's team co-selling. The enterprise channel is the commercial core, and the clinician base is one of its prospecting funnels.
